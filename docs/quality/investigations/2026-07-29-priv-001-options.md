# PRIV-001 — prediction-consensus privacy decision

**Date:** 29 July 2026  
**Source branch:** PR #193 head `901a2bb92b74979283491e5c85d71b01657193a9`  
**Implementation branch:** PR #196  
**Implementation status:** IMPLEMENTED IN DEVELOPMENT — contract 63; production unchanged at 60

## Source findings

### Scope

`public.get_prediction_consensus(uuid)` is tournament-wide. Its cohort is every submitted Original Predictor entry for `p_tournament_id`. It accepts no league identifier, performs no league join and performs no league-membership check.

This is not the private-league post-lock reveal behaviour documented in `docs/design-system.md`. Tournament-wide disclosure therefore requires an explicit small-cohort privacy policy.

### Returned data before suppression

The aggregate JSON object contains:

- `server_now` and `locked_at`;
- `submitted_entries`;
- `champion_race`: up to ten `{team_id, picks}` rows;
- `peoples_final`: up to ten `{team_a_id, team_b_id, picks}` rows;
- `golden_boot`: up to ten `{player_id, team_id, picks}` rows;
- `most_agreed_match`: match/team identifiers, prediction count, home/draw/away counts, dominant outcome and dominant count;
- `most_divided_match`: match/team identifiers, prediction count, home/draw/away counts and the top-two count gap;
- `most_trusted_team`: `{team_id, predicted_wins}`;
- `goals_spread`: minimum, median, maximum, average, number of distinct totals and up to forty `{total_goals, entries}` buckets;
- `only_you`: up to six caller-specific unique champion, final, Golden Boot or exact-score cards.

It returns raw counts rather than percentages. It does not return a complete per-match breakdown or a most-popular exact scoreline.

### Existing eligibility

The function requires:

- an authenticated caller;
- an existing tournament;
- a configured tournament lock;
- current time at or after that lock;
- submitted entries for contributions to the aggregate.

It does not require the caller to have submitted an entry and does not require private-league membership.

### Original inference risk

Before contract 63, one submitted entry was the smallest cohort producing substantive aggregate data. A caller whose own submitted entry contributed could subtract that known prediction from counts; at a cohort of two this could substantially disclose the other entrant. `only_you` also explicitly identified caller selections whose count was one.

## Options considered

### Threshold

| Option | Privacy | Product availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum 5 submitted entries | Leaves as few as four other entries after a caller subtracts their own. Protects less, but suppresses less often in a small closed cohort. | Trends become available earlier. |
| Minimum 10 submitted entries | Leaves at least nine other entries after a contributing caller subtracts their own. This matches the conventional small-cell floor and materially reduces reconstruction risk. | Trends remain suppressed longer in small cohorts. |

### Suppressed response

1. **Empty result:** rejected as ambiguous.
2. **Explicit successful state:** selected. The RPC returns `suppressed: true`, `reason: "not_enough_entries"`, `minimum_entries`, `submitted_entries`, `server_now` and `locked_at`.
3. **Database error:** rejected because expected privacy behaviour must not appear as application failure.

### Caller counting

The caller's submitted entry counts toward the threshold. This keeps one tournament-wide result for every authenticated caller. At ten entries, a contributing caller can remove their own prediction but still leaves nine other entries.

### Configuration

A database constant was selected. Per-competition and runtime-configurable thresholds were rejected because they create inconsistent privacy guarantees and unnecessary mutable security policy.

## Owner decision

The owner approved on PR #193:

- minimum cohort of ten submitted Original Predictor entries;
- caller included;
- database constant;
- explicit successful `not_enough_entries` response below threshold.

## Implementation

Contract 63 migration [`20260729154931_prediction_consensus_minimum_cohort.sql`](../../../supabase/migrations/20260729154931_prediction_consensus_minimum_cohort.sql):

- moves the already-applied contract-61 aggregate into `predictor_internal.get_prediction_consensus_unsuppressed(uuid)`;
- revokes all browser execution on that helper;
- recreates the stable public RPC as the database-owned cohort gate;
- suppresses all aggregate fields below ten entries;
- returns the existing bounded aggregate at ten or above with `suppressed: false`;
- preserves authentication, tournament existence, configured-lock and post-lock checks.

The client maps suppression as a discriminated success state and renders “Not enough entries yet” rather than an error.
