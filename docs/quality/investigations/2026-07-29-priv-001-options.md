# PRIV-001 — prediction-consensus privacy decision

**Date:** 29 July 2026  
**Source branch:** PR #193  
**Implementation branch:** PR #193, with stacked development evidence retained on PR #196  
**Implementation status:** RESOLVED AND PRODUCTION-HOSTED — contract 63

## Source findings

`public.get_prediction_consensus(uuid)` is tournament-wide. Its cohort is every submitted Original Predictor entry for `p_tournament_id`; it is not scoped to a private league.

Before contract 63, one submitted entry was the smallest cohort producing substantive aggregate data. A contributing caller could subtract their own known prediction from raw counts, and caller-specific `only_you` cards could reveal singleton selections.

## Owner decision

The owner approved on PR #193:

- minimum cohort of ten submitted Original Predictor entries;
- caller included in the threshold;
- database constant;
- explicit successful `not_enough_entries` response below threshold.

## Implementation

Contract 63 migration [`20260729154931_prediction_consensus_minimum_cohort.sql`](../../../supabase/migrations/20260729154931_prediction_consensus_minimum_cohort.sql):

- moves the contract-61 aggregate into private `predictor_internal.get_prediction_consensus_unsuppressed(uuid)`;
- revokes browser execution on that helper;
- recreates the public RPC as the database-owned cohort gate;
- suppresses all aggregate fields below ten entries;
- returns the existing bounded aggregate at ten or above with `suppressed: false`;
- preserves authentication, tournament existence, configured-lock and post-lock checks.

The client maps suppression as a discriminated success state and renders “Not enough entries yet” rather than an error.
