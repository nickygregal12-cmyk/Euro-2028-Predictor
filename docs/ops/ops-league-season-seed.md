# League season development seed

**Dated evidence. 5 August 2026.** Records one application of the development
seed and the state it produced. A snapshot; it must keep saying what was true
when it was written.

## What was empty

`Premier League 2026/27` and `Scottish Premiership 2026/27` have existed as
`tournaments` rows since the C1b catalogue migration. On development both were
**completely empty** — zero teams, zero matchweeks, zero fixtures.

That is worth stating plainly because of what it implied. Every season authority
the repository has built reads from those tables: the Match Predictor card and
its locks, matchweek scoring, standings, Last Man Standing eligibility and
round resolution, the Championship round calendar and its launch driver. With
the tables empty, all of it was unreachable code. Nothing could be entered,
locked, scored, settled or displayed, and no surface built on top of it could
have been reviewed.

## What was applied

Generated by `scripts/seed-dev/seed-league-seasons.ts`, applied to development
`iouzoutneyjpugbbtdem` on owner request.

| Season | Clubs | Meetings | Played matchweeks | Post-split | Fixtures |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Premier League 2026/27 | 20 | 2 | 38 | 0 | 380 |
| Scottish Premiership 2026/27 | 12 | 3 | 33 | 5 | 198 |

Both reach a 38 matchweek season by their own route. Twenty clubs meeting twice
is 38 with nothing after it. Twelve clubs meeting three times is 33, and the
split into a top and bottom six adds five more.

Kickoffs run 2026-08-15 11:30Z to 2027-05-03 19:00Z for the Premier League and
2026-08-01 11:30Z to 2027-03-13 12:30Z for the played Scottish rounds.

### The five post-split matchweeks have no fixtures, on purpose

Who meets whom after a split is decided by the table at the split. Those five
rounds therefore exist on the calendar with **no fixtures attached**, and
inventing them would be worse than leaving them out — it would put a definite
calendar where the competition has an open one.

That is a real state rather than a gap. `season_matchweek_lock_at` returns
**null** for a matchweek with no fixtures (confirmed on development for rounds 34
and 38), and contract 109's round resolution already skips a round whose lock
cannot be derived. A matchweek nobody can lock is precisely what an undecided
post-split round is.

### A correction, recorded rather than quietly fixed

The first version of this seed gave the Scottish Premiership a 22 matchweek
double round-robin. That is not the competition: it plays each opponent three
times and then splits. It was corrected on the same day — the season's fixtures
were cleared and regenerated, with nothing else depending on them (no cards, no
jokers, no scores, no submission outcomes, no competition windows, and the
Premier League untouched at 380 fixtures throughout).

The correction changed a claim made about the first version, so that is withdrawn
here rather than left standing. The wrong 22 round calendar left the Scottish
season with 21 future matchweeks, which made `select_season_cup_format` return
`groups` for a twelve-entrant field — and that was written up as a happy accident
giving development one season for contract 111's launch path and one for its
refusal path. With the correct 33 round calendar the season has 32 future
matchweeks and returns `single_group`, the same as the Premier League. **The
refusal path is no longer covered by seeded data**, and exercising it needs a
field larger than twenty entrants rather than a differently shaped season.

## Verified on development, not merely applied

Checked against the database after the write:

| Property | Premier League | Scottish Premiership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meetings per pair | exactly 2 | exactly 3 |
| Times each ordered pair is hosted | exactly 1 | 1 or 2, never 3-0 |
| A club playing twice in a matchweek | 0 | 0 |
| Home fixtures per club | 19-19 | 16-17 |
| Rounds without fixtures | 0 | 5, all labelled post-split |

The 16-17 spread is not a blemish to smooth out. An odd number of meetings
cannot balance a pair, so one club hosts twice and the other once, and each club
ends on `(n-1) + h` home fixtures. That is the Scottish competition as played.

A club playing twice in a matchweek is also refused by
`assert_season_fixture_shape`, so a generator that got it wrong would have been
rejected. The others are the quiet ones: a schedule where some pair never meets,
or is hosted three times by the same club, inserts perfectly happily and is only
noticed — if ever — by a table that stops adding up in March.

Kickoffs are stored from `Europe/London` wall-clock times rather than fixed UTC
offsets, so the August-to-May crossing of BST into GMT is correct. August 12:30
London resolves to 11:30Z and December 15:00 London to 15:00Z, both confirmed.
This is not cosmetic: matchweek locks derive from the earliest kickoff, so an
hour of drift moves when somebody's card shuts.

## The authorities it unblocked

Read-only checks after seeding:

- `season_matchweek_lock_at` resolves for every **played** matchweek in both
  seasons, with the Last Man Standing 30-minute buffer visibly ahead of Match
  Predictor, and returns null for the five post-split rounds.
- `next_eligible_league_round` returns a round for both seasons.
- `select_season_cup_format` for a twelve-entrant field returns `single_group`
  for both — 3 meetings over 33 rounds with a 5-round split for the Premier
  League's 38 future matchweeks, and 2 meetings over 22 rounds with a seeded
  playoff window for the Scottish season's 32.

Scottish matchweek 1 (1 August) is in the past relative to the seeding date, so
it is locked with no predictions and no result, and is inert. If a clean
all-future calendar is wanted, change `firstSaturday` for that season, clear its
fixtures and re-run.

## Re-running

The seed is idempotent and additive. A season that already has fixtures is left
untouched with a notice, and **no path through it deletes a row** — so re-running
cannot double a calendar and cannot destroy one. Re-seeding a season therefore
means clearing it first, deliberately.

```bash
node --experimental-strip-types scripts/seed-dev/seed-league-seasons.ts
```

It only prints SQL; it opens no connection, so running it is never itself the
act of changing a database.

The schedule is generated in SQL, and the TypeScript circle method in the same
file is the oracle it was checked against — the two were compared round for
round for both configured seasons, twelve clubs meeting three times and twenty
meeting twice, and agreed on all 578 fixtures.

`tests/dev/seedLeagueSeasons.test.ts` holds the oracle with property assertions,
including that the two seasons reach 38 matchweeks by their own routes — the
assertion that would have caught the Scottish season being seeded as a 22
matchweek double round-robin. Three mutants were each confirmed to fail the
suite: a dropped home/away alternation, a meeting that does not reverse the one
before, and a rotation that seats a club out.

If the SQL is ever edited, regenerate both and diff them again. A schedule
defect is invisible in the output, because a wrong fixture list looks exactly
like a right one.
